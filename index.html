Werk Nord Control Tower

A control tower for a whole (fictional) German manufacturer. Nineteen specialist copilots report into one system that links their findings into cause chains, scores the health of every department, raises early warnings and runs a governance layer. Code computes every number, AI agents read, route and explain, and a person with the right role approves every decision.

  • Questions are answered by the right specialists in parallel, linked to verified cause chains, and checked so that no invented number survives.
  • A CEO review asks four division leads, who ask 27 departments, and ranks the priorities with safety first.
  • The risk register and control tests fill themselves from the copilots' evidence.
  • What-if analysis shows which fix would have mattered most, with causes that need one, all, or part of their inputs.
  • It proposes new cause links for a person to confirm, ranks root causes by money, forecasts each department's health and simulates scenarios.
  • Meeting slides in one click, a briefing by Teams or email, voice questions, and an English or German interface.

How I work

Three rules run through every project.

Code computes, AI explains

Every number comes from code, so it is exact and testable. Agents read, route and write, and a check removes any number that is not in the evidence.

A person decides

Actions wait for approval by the right role. A too-junior approval is refused by the system, and every decision is logged.

Measure, don't guess

Synthetic data with planted problems and traps shows whether the system finds the real issues and ignores the false ones.
