    Main project

    Werk Nord Control Tower

    A control tower for a whole (fictional) German manufacturer. Nineteen specialist copilots report into one system that links their findings into cause chains, scores the health of every department, raises early warnings and runs a governance layer. Code computes every number, AI agents read, route and explain, and a person with the right role approves every decision.

    • Questions go to the right specialists in parallel, are linked to verified cause chains, and are checked so that no invented number survives.
    • A CEO review asks four division leads, who ask 32 departments, and ranks the priorities with safety first.
    • The risk register and control tests fill themselves from the copilots' evidence.
    • It proposes new cause links for a person to confirm, ranks root causes by money, forecasts health and simulates scenarios.
    • Meeting slides in one click, briefings by Teams or email, voice questions, English or German.
